Training system that protects against manipulation

You will chat with simulated contacts, much like on a messaging app. You can withdraw at any time without giving a reason, and ask for your data to be deleted. Everything runs on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

Each conversation can last up to 45 minutes. A Stop button is always in view, and a researcher is present or reachable ().
